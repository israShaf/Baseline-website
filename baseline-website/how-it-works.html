  1. Connect

    Connect your PDM and ERP with read-only access, or upload BOM exports as spreadsheets or CSV files.

  2. Watch

    Baseline compares the two whenever something is released, and on a regular schedule.

  3. Decide

    When they disagree, the owner gets a clear explanation and a suggested fix to approve.

  4. Confirm

    Baseline checks both systems again and closes the issue only when they match.

Setup happens with you. Before anything goes live, we agree which assemblies to watch, who owns what, and how urgent different kinds of mismatch are.
